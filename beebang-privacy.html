2. In short

The app “BeeBang” does not collect any personal data itself. There is no registration and no user account. Game progress and settings are stored only locally on your device and are not sent to me.

The only data processing arises from the advertising shown in the app (see section 3).

3. Advertising via Google AdMob

To fund the app, ads are shown through Google AdMob. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). Google may also transfer data to Google LLC (USA).

When ads are loaded and displayed, Google may process in particular:

  • your device’s advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and software information (e.g. model, operating system, app version),
  • information about interactions with ads (e.g. impressions, clicks).

This data is used to deliver ads, prevent abuse and fraud, and measure ad performance. I have no influence on how Google processes this data. For more information, see Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Consent and legal basis

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form (Google’s consent management platform, “UMP”) is shown when you first start the app. Data is processed for advertising purposes only if you have given your consent.

The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act), insofar as information on your device is accessed. Transfers to third countries (in particular the USA) rely on the safeguards used by Google, such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

5. Withdrawing your consent

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. To do so, open the app’s settings and choose “Privacy options”. Processing carried out before the withdrawal remains lawful.

In addition, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and limit ad personalization in your Android settings under “Privacy” → “Ads”.

6. Google Play

If you install the app via Google Play, Google processes data in its own responsibility (e.g. account, payment and device information). I have no influence on this. Google’s privacy policy applies.

7. Retention

I do not store any personal data myself. The retention period of data processed by Google is determined by Google. You can delete local game data by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Since I do not store any data about you myself, these rights regarding advertising data generally have to be exercised towards Google. You can also contact me at any time at the email address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority responsible for my place of residence: The Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Der Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit), Ludwig-Erhard-Str. 22, 20459 Hamburg, Germany, phone: +49 40 428 54 4040, email: mailbox@datenschutz.hamburg.de, web: datenschutz-hamburg.de.
